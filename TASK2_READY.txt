STRATEGY SELECTION FRAMEWORK
================================================================================

Based on validation results, decide:

1. BB03 Foundation:
   ✅ ACCEPT if: Negative spread all 4 years, HAC p < 0.05, net return > 0
   ❌ REJECT if: Any year positive, HAC p > 0.10, net return < 0

2. BB01 Breakout:
   ✅ ACCEPT if: Positive spread in 3+ years, stable across periods
   ⚠️ CONDITIONAL if: Positive in 2 years but weak
   ❌ REJECT if: Positive in ≤1 year (unstable)

3. Mean-Reversion Family Independence:
   ✅ DISTINCT if: R² < 0.5 in multivariate regression
   ❌ REDUNDANT if: R² > 0.6 (consolidate to single strategy)
   ⚠️ PARTIAL if: R² between 0.4-0.6 (test both, pick winner)

4. Final Portfolio:
   → 3 strategies minimum
   → 5 strategies maximum
   → Must be geometrically distinct (QR decomposition)
   → Must have temporal stability (yearly consistency)
